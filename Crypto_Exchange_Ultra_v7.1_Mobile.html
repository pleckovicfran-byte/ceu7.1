Most Asked Questions

Quick answers about using Crypto Exchange Ultra.

Does Crypto Exchange Ultra really have no fees?
Crypto Exchange Ultra does not charge trading, deposit or withdrawal fees inside the app. The app is a portfolio and trading simulation, so it does not process real blockchain transfers or charge blockchain network fees. External services you use separately may have their own fees.
Is Crypto Exchange Ultra a real cryptocurrency exchange?
No. It is a cryptocurrency portfolio and exchange simulator. Prices may be displayed for reference, but trades and balance changes inside the app are simulated and do not take place on a real exchange.
Can I send cryptocurrency to a real wallet?
No. The Send feature records a simulated transaction in your app. It does not broadcast a transaction to a blockchain or move real cryptocurrency.
How do I create an account?
Choose Create account on the sign-in screen and complete all required profile fields. Then choose a unique username and a password with at least 8 characters.
Do I log in with my email or username?
You log in with your username and password. Your email address is saved as part of your profile, but it is not your sign-in username.
Can I use the same account on different devices?
Yes. Sign in with the same username and password. Your cloud-saved portfolio and transaction history are synchronized through your account.
Why are cryptocurrency prices changing?
The Market section displays changing reference prices. Your portfolio's estimated value can change when those prices update, even if your coin quantities stay the same.
What does Pending Transactions mean?
It lists simulated transfers that have not yet been marked as completed in the app. It does not mean a real blockchain transaction is waiting to be confirmed.
Can I delete my account?
If you would like to delete your account, please contact us at pleckovicfran@gmail.com. Our team will assist you with your request.
What should I do if I forget my password?
Password reset is not currently available on the sign-in screen. Please contact us at pleckovicfran@gmail.com for assistance.
